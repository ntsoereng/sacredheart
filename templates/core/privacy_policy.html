Your information matters

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026

Our commitment

Sacred Heart High School respects your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We collect only information needed for legitimate school purposes, limit access to authorised people, and use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it.

1. Who we are

Sacred Heart High School is responsible for deciding why and how personal information submitted through this website is used. This policy applies to school applications, contact messages, alumni submissions, staff accounts, and ordinary use of this website.

2. Information we collect

Admissions: academic year; the learner’s name, surname, date of birth, nationality, home address, district, previous school, and candidate number where recorded; the parent or guardian’s name, telephone number, and email address; submission time, application reference, review status, reviewer details, and internal review notes.

Contact enquiries: name, email address, subject, message, submission time, and whether staff have reviewed the message.

Alumni Network: name, graduation year, private contact details, location, occupation, industry, photograph, profile summary, memories, advice to learners, mentorship preferences, publication consent, verification status, and private staff notes.

Mentorship requests: a learner or recent graduate’s name, private contact details, requested focus area, preferred mentor, goals, consent, handling status, and private staff notes.

Alumni opportunities: the verified contributor, opportunity type, title, provider, eligibility summary, application link, deadline, review status, and private staff notes.

Staff accounts: authentication and account information needed to operate the secure staff portal and maintain an audit trail.

Technical and security information: IP address and limited server, browser, request, session, and timing information needed to deliver pages, maintain security, remember a recent application reference, and diagnose faults. For admissions, we also create one-time submission-token records and keyed, non-readable identifiers derived from the requester’s IP address and the guardian email address or telephone number. These identifiers help count submission attempts without storing the contact details in the rate-limit record.

We do not use this website to sell behavioural advertising profiles.

3. Why we use information

  • To receive, identify, review, and communicate about school applications.
  • To identify duplicate applications, limit repeated submission attempts, reject invalid or reused submission tokens, and protect the application service from automated misuse.
  • To respond to questions and messages sent to the school.
  • To verify and publish consented alumni directory profiles.
  • To review opportunities submitted by verified alumni before publication.
  • To assess mentorship requests and safely coordinate suitable introductions between learners or recent graduates and alumni.
  • To administer the website, authenticate staff, prevent misuse, maintain records, and protect school systems.
  • To comply with applicable law and protect the rights, safety, and legitimate interests of learners, families, staff, alumni, and the school.

We use consent where publication, contact, or another optional use requires it, and otherwise process information where needed for the requested school service, legitimate school administration, safeguarding, or legal obligations.

Security checks may automatically stop or delay a submission when an attempt limit is reached or a submission token is invalid, expired, or has already been used. These checks protect the service and do not decide whether a learner should be admitted. If you believe a genuine application was blocked, please contact the school.

4. Children and learner information

Admissions and mentorship information may concern a person under 18 and should be submitted by, or with the knowledge and appropriate authority of, a parent or legal guardian. We handle children’s information with additional care, restrict access to authorised staff and appropriate safeguarding purposes, do not publish application or mentorship request information, and coordinate any alumni introduction through the school.

5. Alumni publication, mentorship, and private contacts

An alumni directory profile and photograph become public only after staff verification and when publication consent has been provided. Alumni email addresses, telephone numbers, mentorship requests, staff notes, and reviewer details remain private. Mentorship introductions are coordinated by the school; submitting a request does not guarantee a match. An alumnus may withdraw publication or mentorship consent by contacting us, and we will update future use within a reasonable period, subject to lawful recordkeeping and safeguarding needs.

6. No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not give it to advertisers or data brokers. We may share only what is reasonably necessary with authorised school staff, vetted service providers working for the school, professional advisers, authorities where lawfully required, or emergency and safeguarding bodies where necessary to protect someone. Service providers must use information only for the agreed service and protect it appropriately.

7. Security and incident response

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information, including restricted staff access, authenticated administration, access records, secure configuration, updates, backups, and staff confidentiality practices. No internet service can honestly guarantee absolute security. If we identify a breach, we will act to contain and investigate it, reduce harm, restore security, and notify affected people or authorities where required by applicable law.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as reasonably required for the purpose collected, school recordkeeping, safeguarding, dispute handling, security, and legal obligations. This includes retaining application records for the admissions process and related recordkeeping, and retaining submission-token and rate-limit records for as long as reasonably needed to prevent misuse, investigate incidents, and maintain the integrity of the application service. Unsuccessful, withdrawn, rejected, outdated, expired, or no-longer-needed records are periodically reviewed for secure deletion or de-identification. Published alumni profiles remain available while consent continues and the profile remains appropriate for the Alumni Network.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and appropriate identity checks, you may ask whether we hold your information, request access, correct inaccurate or incomplete information, object to or restrict certain use, withdraw consent, or request deletion where the school has no lawful reason to retain it. A parent or guardian may exercise appropriate rights concerning a child’s application. We will respond within a reasonable time and explain if a legal or safeguarding obligation prevents us from fulfilling all or part of a request.

10. International services and external links

Some technology providers may process or store information outside Lesotho. Where this occurs, we will seek appropriate contractual, organisational, and technical safeguards consistent with applicable requirements. This site may link to external services; their privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators.

11. Legal framework and changes

We aim to process personal information consistently with the information-protection principles in Lesotho’s Data Protection Act, 2011 (Act No. 5 of 2012), and other applicable requirements. We may update this policy as services or legal duties change. Material changes will be identified by a revised effective date and, where appropriate, an additional website notice.
