Your information matters

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 August 2026

Our commitment

Sacred Heart High School respects your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We collect only information needed for legitimate school purposes, limit access to authorised people, and use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it.

1. Who we are

Sacred Heart High School is responsible for deciding why and how personal information submitted through this website is used. This policy applies to school applications, contact messages, alumni submissions and profile-update verification, staff accounts, essential cookies, and ordinary use of this website.

2. Information we collect

Admissions: academic year; the learner’s name, surname, date of birth, nationality, home address, district, previous school, and candidate number where recorded; the parent or guardian’s name, telephone number, and email address; submission time, application reference, review status, reviewer details, and internal review notes.

Contact enquiries: name, email address, subject, message, submission time, and whether staff have reviewed the message.

Alumni Network: name, graduation year, private contact details, location, occupation, industry, photograph, profile summary, memories, advice to learners, publication consent, verification status, profile-update requests, and private staff notes. For a profile-update verification, we also record a non-readable digest of the one-time link, when it was created and expires, whether it was used, and limited security information about the request.

Alumni opportunities: the verified contributor, opportunity type, title, provider, eligibility summary, application link, deadline, review status, and private staff notes.

Staff accounts: authentication and account information needed to operate the secure staff portal and maintain an audit trail.

Technical and security information: IP address and limited server, browser, request, session, cookie, and timing information needed to deliver pages, maintain security, remember a recent application reference, and diagnose faults. For public forms, we also create one-time submission-token records and keyed, non-readable identifiers derived from the requester’s IP address and any supplied email address or telephone number. These identifiers help count submission attempts without storing the contact details in the rate-limit record.

We do not use this website to sell behavioural advertising profiles.

3. Why we use information

  • To receive, identify, review, and communicate about school applications.
  • To identify duplicate applications, limit repeated public-form attempts, reject invalid or reused submission tokens, and protect the website from automated misuse.
  • To respond to questions and messages sent to the school.
  • To verify and publish consented alumni directory profiles, privately confirm control of an address already on record, and review requested corrections.
  • To review opportunities submitted by verified alumni before publication.
  • To administer the website, authenticate staff, prevent misuse, maintain records, and protect school systems.
  • To comply with applicable law and protect the rights, safety, and legitimate interests of learners, families, staff, alumni, and the school.

We use consent where publication, contact, or another optional use requires it, and otherwise process information where needed for the requested school service, legitimate school administration, safeguarding, or legal obligations.

Security checks may automatically stop or delay a public-form submission when an attempt limit is reached or a submission token is invalid, expired, or has already been used. These checks protect the service and do not decide whether a learner should be admitted, whether an alumni submission should be approved, or whether staff access should be granted. If you believe a genuine submission was blocked, please contact the school.

4. Children and learner information

Admissions information may concern a person under 18 and should be submitted by, or with the knowledge and appropriate authority of, a parent or legal guardian. We handle children’s information with additional care, restrict access to authorised staff and appropriate safeguarding purposes, and do not publish application information.

5. Alumni publication and private-email verification

An alumni directory profile and photograph become public only after staff verification and when publication consent has been provided. Alumni email addresses, telephone numbers, staff notes, reviewer details, and correction requests remain private. An alumnus may withdraw publication consent by contacting us, and we will update future use within a reasonable period, subject to lawful recordkeeping and safeguarding needs.

When someone requests a profile correction, the supplied address is compared privately with the address already held for that profile. The website gives the same response whether or not it matches. A verification record and email are created only when the address matches, subject to security and sending limits.

The email contains a random, single-use link that expires after one hour. The raw link token is not stored in our database; we retain only a non-readable digest used to validate it. Following the link confirms control of the mailbox but does not automatically alter the public profile. Authorised staff review every requested correction before publication.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses only the first-party cookies needed to provide and secure its services:

  • csrftoken — CSRF security: a random security value that helps prevent another website from submitting a form in your name. It may remain for up to approximately one year.
  • sessionid — session: a random identifier for information stored securely on our server. It supports staff sign-in, confirmation messages, and limited recent form state. It does not contain the submitted application or alumni profile itself and expires after up to eight hours.

In production these cookies are restricted to secure connections, protected from ordinary script access, and sent with same-site protections. We do not currently set analytics, advertising, personalisation, or social-media tracking cookies, and we do not use cookies to follow visitors across unrelated websites. Blocking essential cookies may prevent forms, verification, or staff sign-in from working correctly.

The Contact page may display an embedded Google Map. When that page loads the map, the browser connects to Google, which may receive technical information and use cookies under its own privacy terms. The map is not required to use our forms or the alumni verification service.

7. No sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information. We do not give it to advertisers or data brokers. We may share only what is reasonably necessary with authorised school staff, vetted service providers working for the school, professional advisers, authorities where lawfully required, or emergency and safeguarding bodies where necessary to protect someone. Service providers must use information only for the agreed service and protect it appropriately.

8. Security and incident response

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information, including restricted staff access, authenticated administration, access records, secure configuration, updates, backups, and staff confidentiality practices. No internet service can honestly guarantee absolute security. If we identify a breach, we will act to contain and investigate it, reduce harm, restore security, and notify affected people or authorities where required by applicable law.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as reasonably required for the purpose collected, school recordkeeping, safeguarding, dispute handling, security, and legal obligations. Alumni verification links expire after one hour and cannot be reused; their digest records may be retained for a limited period where reasonably needed to enforce sending limits, investigate misuse, and demonstrate the verification history. We likewise retain submission-token and rate-limit records only as reasonably needed to prevent misuse and maintain the integrity of public forms. Unsuccessful, withdrawn, rejected, outdated, expired, or no-longer-needed records are periodically reviewed for secure deletion or de-identification. Published alumni profiles remain available while consent continues and the profile remains appropriate for the Alumni Network.

10. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and appropriate identity checks, you may ask whether we hold your information, request access, correct inaccurate or incomplete information, object to or restrict certain use, withdraw consent, or request deletion where the school has no lawful reason to retain it. A parent or guardian may exercise appropriate rights concerning a child’s application. We will respond within a reasonable time and explain if a legal or safeguarding obligation prevents us from fulfilling all or part of a request.

11. Service providers, international services, and external links

We use service providers for website hosting and email delivery. To deliver an alumni verification message, the school’s mail provider processes the recipient address, sender and delivery details, and message content. Some technology providers may process or store information outside Lesotho. Where this occurs, we will seek appropriate contractual, organisational, and technical safeguards consistent with applicable requirements. This site also uses externally hosted font files, may display an embedded Google Map, and may link to external services; their privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators.

12. Legal framework and changes

We aim to process personal information consistently with the information-protection principles in Lesotho’s Data Protection Act, 2011 (Act No. 5 of 2012), and other applicable requirements. We may update this policy as services or legal duties change. Material changes will be identified by a revised effective date and, where appropriate, an additional website notice.
